# DIM Digital Hub

MASTER PROMPT — DIGITAL INTELLIGENCE MARKETPLACE FRONT WEBSITE

ROLE

You are a senior product designer, UX strategist, conversion specialist, brand designer, and full-stack frontend engineer.

Your task is to transform the pre-authentication experience of Digital Intelligence Marketplace (DIM) into a complete, premium, conversion-focused company website that appears BEFORE a user signs in or creates an account.

This website is NOT simply a landing page.

It is the public-facing digital home of Digital Intelligence Marketplace.

The experience must clearly communicate:

What DIM is.
Why DIM exists.
Who DIM is for.
What users can do on DIM.
How DIM helps people progress.
What each membership provides.
Why someone should join.
And what the next step is.

The final experience should make joining DIM feel like the natural next step after exploring the platform.

1. CORE DIM POSITIONING

Digital Intelligence Marketplace is a next-generation digital intelligence ecosystem designed to help individuals participate successfully in the AI-powered digital economy.

The website must communicate this distinction immediately.

2. PRIMARY WEBSITE OBJECTIVE

The website has one primary business objective:

Convert visitors into DIM members.

However, do this through:

trust

DO NOT use deceptive dark patterns.

DO NOT hide pricing.

DO NOT create fake urgency.

DO NOT make users believe they have to subscribe when they do not.

Instead, make visitors think:

"This platform was built for someone like me."

Then:

"I want to see what I can access."

"I should create my free DIM account."

The preferred conversion path is:

VISITOR ↓ DISCOVER DIM ↓ UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM ↓ DISCOVER THE ECOSYSTEM ↓ IDENTIFY THEIR JOURNEY ↓ EXPLORE FEATURES ↓ COMPARE MEMBERSHIPS ↓ SEE THE COMMUNITY / OPPORTUNITIES ↓ CREATE FREE ACCOUNT ↓ ONBOARDING ↓ PERSONALIZED DIM EXPERIENCE

3. IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULE

The public website must be completely separate from authenticated application functionality.

A visitor who has not signed in should see the public DIM website.

Once the user signs in:

→ take them into the actual DIM application.

Do NOT expose the authenticated dashboard as the default homepage for logged-out visitors.

Architecture:

PUBLIC WEBSITE / ├── Home ├── About DIM ├── How DIM Works ├── Learn ├── Build ├── Connect ├── Earn ├── Grow ├── AI Tools ├── Career ├── Creator ├── Community ├── Membership ├── 8-Month Career Program ├── Opportunities ├── FAQ ├── Contact ├── Sign In └── Create Account

AUTHENTICATED APP / ├── Dashboard ├── Learning Hub ├── AI Tools Directory ├── Community ├── Marketplace ├── Career ├── Creator └── Profile

Never mix these experiences unnecessarily.
